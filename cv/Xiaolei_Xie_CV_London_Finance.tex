\section{Professional Experience}

\job{London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG)}{London}{Senior Quantitative
  Modeller}{Aug 2021 -- Present} 
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Yield Book analytics:} Research and develop pricing and analytics
  for fixed-income, FX and mortgage instruments, with primary responsibility
  for fixed income and FX.
\item \textbf{Equity volatility modelling:} Implemented a Heston equity-volatility
  calibration engine in C++/Linux to supply volatility inputs for convertible-bond
  pricing. It estimates parameters from market prices of European options
  using GSL for multidimensional optimisation and numerical integration
  and OpenMP for parallel execution. The resulting surfaces matched
  market quotes materially better than the production SSVI model, particularly
  at the money, while calibrating in 2--5 seconds on a four-core 2.4~GHz
  server.
\item \textbf{Illiquid-equity volatility:} Built a Python/PCA framework
  to infer volatility surfaces for equities without liquid options from
  correlated equities with calibrated Heston surfaces; used NumPy, SciPy
  and SQLite.
\item \textbf{ETF pricing performance:} Refactored and parallelised
  legacy C pricing code for fixed-income and mortgage ETFs with
  10,000+ constituents, using multi-process pricing servers, modern
  C++ and cereal serialisation to support real-time analytics.
\item \textbf{Delivery and leadership:} Mentor new joiners, answer customer
  questions about Yield Book analytics, calculation methods and performance,
  and deliver enhancements and fixes arising from internal model
  validation and customer feedback.
\end{itemize}
\job{Lloyds Bank}{London}{Quantitative Analyst -- Model Validation}{Oct
  2018 -- Aug 2021} 
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Retail sales forecasting:} Led a team of three and worked
  with bank economists to build one-month-ahead forecasts for UK Retail
  Sales Index components using linear regression, ARMA models and Met
  Office weather data; implemented in Python with NumPy, SciPy, statsmodels
  and SQLite.
\item \textbf{Interest-rate stress testing:} Designed a mathematically grounded
  framework to quantify portfolio losses from yield-curve moves, modelling
  selected curve points with a constant copula and scenario-dependent
  marginal distributions. 
\end{itemize}
